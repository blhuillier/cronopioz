<div class="sq-row"><div class="sq-col sq-pad" style="--span:1"></div><div class="sq-col" style="--span:10"><div class="text"><p>Sung is an artist-researcher materializing fractures of control at the seams of body, technology, and nature. Tensions are shaped into tactile hybrid forms through iterative analog–digital processes and custom-made tools. Her practice blends material experimentation with a decade of research building immersive systems for embodied sensemaking and augmented creativity. </p><p>Current projects include <em>The Martian Gardener Project</em><a href="/files/SungPortfolio2026v2_EN.pdf" rel="noopener" target="_blank">,</a> which displaces the human–plant relationship off-world to imagine a different rhythm of care, and <em>Injury Atlas: The Dossiers</em>, which stages what gets erased, compressed, and rewritten when a lived body is rendered as clinical data. </p><p>Her work has been supported by Korea Foundation for the Advancement of Science and Creativity (KOFAC) and the Seoul Foundation for Arts &amp; Culture; her collaborative projects are permanently installed at Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP) and the Gwangju National Science Museum. </p><p>sung@cronopioz.com /  pronounced: suhng-ah-jahng / CV available upon request</p></div></div><div class="sq-col sq-pad" style="--span:1"></div></div><hr><div class="sq-row"><div class="sq-col" style="--span:2"><div class="text"><p class="ta-center">EDUCATION</p></div></div><div class="sq-col" style="--span:10"><div class="text"><p>Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh, PA, U.S. <br/>Ph.D. studies in Human-Computer Interaction Institute (HCII)</p><p>Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST), Daejeon, Korea<br/>M.S. in Culture Technology | Interactive Media &amp;
