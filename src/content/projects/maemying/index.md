The crank pulls the arched plastic inward which then springs back into shape, mimicking how cicadas use their muscles to engage two elastic membranes ('tymbals') and its clicking ribs. The prototype was adapted into complex designs and sculptural works that have been exhibited in various venues.</p><p>Materials: Acryl, MC gears, motion sensor, mini motors, paper clips,
