The event is an outgrowth of a community building project of HackerSpace Seoul to creatively engage our community and shed the negative connotations associated with the word "hack". Contestants are presented with an abundance of a single type of material - the "secret ingredient" - with which they would have to "hack", as in use to build something meaningful or interesting within 2 hours. Jury members then decide the finalists and the audience vote on the best hacks.</p><p><br/>Through hacking, we motivate people to question what things are and what they could be, and how everyday materials can be recreated into something completely new. Past ingredients include tape, wooden chopsticks, and most recently zip ties.</p></div></div><div class="sq-col sq-pad" style="--span:2"></div></div><div class="sq-row"><div class="sq-col sq-pad" style="--span:2"></div><div class="sq-col" style="--span:8"><div class="text"><ul><li><p>11.2012 | Iron Hack Workshop for Kids, sponsored by Hanyang University </p></li><li><p>7.2012 | Iron Hack II, sponsored by Seogyo Art Space, Grand Prize: RepRap 3D printer donated by OpenCreators</p></li><li><p>4.2012 | Iron Hack China, sponsored by Beijing Maker Carnival, World Class University (WCU)</p></li><li><p>12.2011 | Iron Hack I, sponsored by Platoon Kunsthalle, Make:Korea, WCU, KOFAC (Korean Foundation for the Advancement of Science and Creativity)</p></li></ul></div></div><div class="sq-col sq-pad" style="--span:2"></div></div><figure><img src="/images/ironhack/wvmoid8w3lc0zgs79t0w-p1_page_04_image_0001.webp" alt="" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="621" height="367"></figure><div class="gallery gallery-grid" style="--cols:3"><figure><img src="/images/ironhack/29xlowi14ucq17aaud3o-p1_page_04_image_0005.webp" alt="" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="483" height="427"></figure><figure><img src="/images/ironhack/0z095papqnsii5l7khtf-p1_page_04_image_0004.webp" alt="" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="513" height="427"></figure><figure><img src="/images/ironhack/tuo27t6ee2a5jb9q31uw-p1_page_04_image_0002.webp" alt="" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="463" height="423"></figure></div>
