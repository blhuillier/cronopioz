Say goodbye to sifting through cluttered AI conversations—now, you can manage them smoothly and efficiently.</strong></p><p>ChatCrawl lets you bookmark, pin, and tag chat sessions for quick access and easy retrieval.  You can toggle individual questions to view only the responses you care about, and easily resume conversations at any time. A dedicated Collections page organizes your bookmarks, while the History page gives a complete overview of past chats—sortable by recent activity, interaction frequency, or chronological order.</p><p>Planned enhancements include highlighting and saving specific chat segments, viewing AI-generated summaries, and importing official ChatGPT data for unified management.
