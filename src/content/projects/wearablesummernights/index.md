"Animal vaccine" was the overarching theme of the works, which were commissioned by a German vaccine company for an outdoor garden event in Jeju-do. Because the event took place at night, the sound sensitive, illuminating accents made the pieces alluring and inspiring. The works were mostly made of recycled materials such as plastic cups, leftover paperboards and fabric, and disposable syringes used for acrylic solvent glues.<br/> </p><p>Aug 4, 2012 | Fashion Technology Prototyping Workshop, Smart Cloud Conference, COEX<br/>Aug 2-4, 2012 | Showcased in Smart Cloud Show Exhibition, COEX<br/>Jun 11, 2012 | Showcased in Boehringer-Ingelheim Outdoor Garden Dinner Party, Shilla Jeju Hotel</p><p>Vaccine Helmet &amp; Sinister Cape - Collaboration with Zachary G.
