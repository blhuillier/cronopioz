It was created as permanent exhibition content for the Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP) Playground, which opened in March 2014. </p><p>The Soribyeol experience is divided into several stages, during which the children bring the sphere(star) to life with their voices. They stir the star awake, paint the star, and create lifeforms to inhabit it with their singing. I developed the core system module that uses their singing voices as input to generate organic forms in different colors, shapes, sizes and movement according to its point of origin and acoustic properties.</p></div><div class="video" style="aspect-ratio:100 / 56.25"><iframe src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/245700521" title="ddp0_1" loading="lazy" allow="autoplay; Each mic generates a different type of creature with a visually distinct shape and motion. The louder the sound, the more creatures that come into being. </p><p><strong>The four agent types are also symbolic of the four classical elements.</strong> <strong>Fish swim in water, moths (insects) flutter their wings and gather around fire, birds soar and glide in the air, and four-legged creatures (mammals) roam the earth.
