<div class="text"><p class="ta-center">2013</p><p>Depending on how many agents are in the ecosystem, a changing fraction of those that collide into each other engage in mating. During mating their hearts grow bigger, body shapes mutate and colors change while spinning, and a small fraction of those produce offspring. Their original shape, size and colors are restored after mating. Newborns grow until they reach a certain size.
