<div class="sq-row"><div class="sq-col sq-pad" style="--span:1"></div><div class="sq-col" style="--span:10"><div class="text"><p class="ta-center">2017–2018 · Interactive Hologram Installation<br/>On permanent display at <a href="https://www.sciencecenter.or.kr/kor/main.do" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Gwangju National Science Museum</a> (<a href="https://www.sciencecenter.or.kr/eng/menu/sub.do?menuId=134" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Sci-Art Zone</a>)</p><p>Sound of Light turns the visible spectrum into an interactive field of color and sound. Seven orbs hover in holographic space, each reacting to an airtap with its own audiovisual rhythm. When visitors activate multiple orbs, their responses layer and continually reconfigure, creating an experience that changes from moment to moment. The piece combines sonified light wavelengths, holographic imagery, and a multi-user gesture interface that supports shared exploration.</p><p>As lead researcher of the <a href="https://kct.gist.ac.kr/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">KCT</a> project, I co-developed the conceptual direction, shaping the scientific basis of the work into its visual and sonic language and designing the interaction that defines the experience. </p></div></div><div class="sq-col sq-pad" style="--span:1"></div></div><div class="sq-row"><div class="sq-col sq-pad"
